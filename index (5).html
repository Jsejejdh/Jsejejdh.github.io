Dino Game Jump Controller

Play the Chrome Dino game by jumping in real life. The webcam tracks your body with pose detection, and when your shoulder moves up past a set threshold, the program presses the spacebar so the Dino jumps. A short cooldown stops one movement from triggering repeated jumps.

Runs on a small 320x240 frame with the fastest pose model, so it stays smooth, and shows a live webcam preview.
